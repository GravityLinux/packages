Proton (ARM64, Gravity) is Valve's Proton 11.0 (tag proton-11.0-2e) built for
AArch64 from public sources with two changes:

- Wine: "ntdll: Do not write predicates into a header-only SVE signal
  context". It fixes rt_sigreturn failures on CPUs with SME but no SVE
  (Apple M4). This change was generated by an LLM.
- FEX: upstream commit 681636cd68ea7d7083635479a9211651dcec6d07 by Ryan
  Houdek, applied unchanged.

Both changes as patches, the exact source revisions and the build
instructions are in PROTON-SOURCE.md of the steam package
(/usr/share/doc/steam) and in https://github.com/GravityLinux/packages under
steam/. The licenses are those of Proton and its components: see LICENSE and
LICENSE.OFL in this directory.

The steam launcher downloaded this tool at the first start. The
steam-gravity-proton-default package makes it the default for games that
have no other choice.
